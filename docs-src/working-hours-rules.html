Working Hours Rules 工时计算规则

HDL Delivery Kitchen · Effective from 16 Nov 2026 · Minimum unit 0.25h · 最小单位 0.25 小时

1 Refunds 退款

Valid refund (each order)
有效退款，每单
−0.25h
Simple missing item, when the site's inaccuracy rate is above 4%
站点不准确率超过 4% 时，简单漏放（每单）
−1h
Zero refunds in the week
一周零退款
+0.5h
  • Refunds without a valid reason are not counted. 没有合理原因的退款不计入。
  • Refunds are matched to the shift at the time of the order and updated the following week. 按下单时间对应当班班次，下一周更新。

2 Stock & Food Safety 库存与食品安全

Expired product (including expired date labels) or severely spoiled food, each item
过期产品（含过期日期标签）或严重变质，每项
1.5h
Not following FIFO, product not fully defrosted when needed, or fryer oil not changed, each item
未遵守先进先出、需要时未完全解冻、油锅的油未更换，每项
1h
  • Finder: + the same hours. Send a photo with the date found. 发现人加相同工时，需发照片并注明日期。
  • Previous shift at that site: − the same hours, split equally if more than one person was on shift. 该站点上一班次的人扣相同工时，多人在班则平分。
  • Issues found during a manager check apply to the previous shift in the same way. 管理检查时发现的问题，同样计入上一班次。

3 Attendance 出勤

Missed shift (cover required), per shift
缺勤（需他人顶班），每班
−1.5h
Covering a colleague's shift
替同事顶班
+1.5h
Late, reported in advance, up to 15 mins
提前报备，迟到 15 分钟内
0
15–30 mins late
迟到 15–30 分钟
−0.25h
30 mins – 2 hours late
迟到 30 分钟 – 2 小时
−0.5h
2–4 hours late
迟到 2–4 小时
−1h
More than 4 hours late
迟到 4 小时以上
−2h

4 Good to know 说明

  • Adjustments are shown on your weekly hours in the Rota page. 所有调整会显示在 Rota 页面的每周工时里。
  • If anything looks wrong, just let your manager know and we will check it together. 如果有疑问，随时告诉管理人员，我们一起核对。
